To use a decision guide effectively, users should:

Identify Goals: Clearly define what they want to achieve, such as data ingestion, transformation, or real-time processing.
Assess Technical Skills: Determine whether they prefer no-code, low-code, or code-based solutions.
Understand Data Workloads: Evaluate the type of data workload they are dealing with (e.g., batch, real-time, etc.).
Consider Transformation Needs: Identify the complexity of data transformations required for their projects.
By systematically addressing these aspects, users can leverage the decision guides to choose the most suitable services and tools within Microsoft Fabric for their specific scenarios.
